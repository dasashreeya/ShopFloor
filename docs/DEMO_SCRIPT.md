# Live demo: "Casa Elena" (about 3 minutes)

**The story.** Elena runs a family Mexican restaurant with a catering side in Riverdale Park, MD (a composite persona
built from our market study: phone is still ~40% of takeout ordering, ~62% of small-business calls go unanswered, and 59% of
small businesses have invoices 30+ days late). Friday nights the phone rings off the hook, 45 Google reviews a month sit
unanswered, catering clients pay late, and her inbox is a mess. Half her customers speak Spanish. She can invest $700/month
*if it pays off*.

**AI usage:** the whole swipe demo uses **zero AI calls**; recommendations are deterministic math. Only the optional
"Try it on your own example" box calls an AI model (limited to 20 per hour per device), so use it **once at most**, or skip it.

## Before you present
1. Open the deployed link in a **private/incognito window** (clean start), on a phone or with the browser at phone width.
2. Optional: open the same link in a second tab, already on the **How we rate** tab, for the research segment.

- **Tap any card (or ⓘ):** real plans with the source link and date, ratings per platform, verbatim reviews including the bad ones, and "excluded as off-topic" reviews with the reason.

## If a judge asks "is this real?"
- Prices, plans, trials, contract terms and reviews: real, linked and dated (Nov 8, 2026).
- Elena is a composite persona; her numbers come from the market study, and anyone can type their own.
- "Try it" replies are a labeled simulation of the vendor's described behavior, not the vendor's product.
