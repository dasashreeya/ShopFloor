## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for independent SMB operators—restaurateurs, retail managers, and local service providers—who need immediate operational leverage without tech jargon. It blends industrial-grade reliability with the tactile responsiveness of modern operational fintech. 

The aesthetic is grounded, authoritative, and momentum-driven. The design movement marries **Tactile Utility** with **High-Contrast Precision**:
- **Industrial Dependability:** Deep naval slates evoke steel infrastructure, machinery control panels, and serious enterprise reliability.
- **Kinetic Direction:** Safety-amber vectors bring physical urgency and clarity to decision paths, financial upside, and automated workforce status.
- **Zero Ambiguity:** Complex AI concepts are translated into tangible workplace metaphors—"Shadow Shifts", "Decision Decks", "Roster Cards", and "One-Click Fire" switches. The operator feels completely in control of an autonomous workforce rather than overwhelmed by algorithms.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate hierarchy between base authority and kinetic action:

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Navy Foundry):** Anchors high-priority headers, structural navigation bars, bottom tab docks, and primary terminal cards. Paired with elevated container tiers `#132238` and `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary (`#FF8A00` - Industrial Safety Amber):** The definitive action vector derived from the brand mark. Reserved for high-value conversion points, ROI upward trajectory indicators, hiring confirmations, and live status pulses. Supported by `#F59E0B` for hover/warning states and `#FFFBEB` for tint backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Verified Mint):** Applied strictly to validated outcomes, 90-day replay proofs, positive cash flow metrics, and golden benchmark certifications.
- **Destructive/Safety Switch (`#EF4444` - Emergency Red):** Used exclusively for the "One-Click Fire" failsafe and irreversible disconnection actions.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:** Pure canvas `#FFFFFF` and structured slate tiers (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`) provide crisp data backplates, while `#64748B` and `#334155` maintain legible metadata and secondary labels.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a paired industrial architecture:

- **Display & Financial Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers precise, mechanical personality for impact cards, hero counters, and data tables. Monospaced characteristics and tabular figures (`tnum`) ensure numbers align flush in prediction models (`+$1,140/mo`, `$29/mo`, `99.4%`).
- **Body & Operational Copy (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean humanist geometric readability. Ensures long-form operational logs, candidate capabilities, and audit details remain legible under quick-glance field conditions.
- **Badge & Metric Micro-copy:** Uppercase tracked `Space Grotesk` (`0.06em`) formats all benchmark badges, golden-set verification stamps, and real-time sensor dials.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, optimizing for single-thumb decision-making on the floor, while reflowing smoothly into multi-column control rooms on tablet and desktop terminals:

- **Mobile Viewport (<768px):** Single vertical stack with fixed bottom safe-area navigation dock (64px height + device safe bottom). Fluid 4-column structure with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Primary decision cards span full width minus margins.
- **Tablet / Split Viewport (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and gutters. Reflows candidate profile into side-by-side backtest analytics and live control pane.
- **Desktop Command Dashboard (>1024px):** 12-column grid pinned to max-width 1360px with `2.5rem` margins. The Decision Deck sits center-stage (spanning 5 columns) flanked by the Active Roster (3 columns) and Real-Time Replay Benchmarks (4 columns).
- **Rhythm Principle:** Internal card padding scales strictly between `space-md` (compact cards) and `space-lg` (decision deck primary face). Micro-metadata pairs use `space-xs`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, floating cloud shadows in favor of crisp, engineered mechanical elevation:

- **Level 0 (Floor/Canvas):** Default background (`#F8FAFC`), crisp flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Roster Cards):** Flat `#FFFFFF` fill with a precision border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. No ambient blur; structural clarity.
- **Level 2 (Active Decision Deck Top Card):** Raised interactive surface. Elevated with dual physical shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 10px 24px -3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12)` accompanied by a crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` edge.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Gesture Drag Overlays):** Deep navy translucent backdrops (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Industrial Inset (Metric Wells):** Sunken data cells utilize an inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`) with `#F1F5F9` background to mimic physical instrument readouts.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines ergonomic rounded edges with disciplined industrial structure:

- **Corner Radius Scale:**
  - Base components (inputs, list items, small buttons): `0.5rem` (`rounded`).
  - Cards, Decision Deck items, and modal dialogs: `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
  - Hero Deck wrappers: `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Filter chips, confidence gauge indicators, and gesture directional pills use fully rounded capsule shapes (`9999px`) to immediately communicate swipe and tap affordances.

## Components

### 1. Decision Deck Cards
- **Architecture:** Stacked physics-enabled deck with 3 visible tiers. Top card features worker avatar/specifier, primary role title, pricing tag (`$29/mo`), and estimated impact banner.
- **Gesture Anchors:** 
  - Swipe Left: Subtle slate tint with red tag: "Pass".
  - Swipe Up: Amber halo border with tag: "14-Day Shadow Shift".
  - Swipe Right: Green halo border with tag: "Hire to Floor".
- **Footer Controls:** Explicit tap fallback buttons below deck (Cross, Clock/Shadow, Checkmark) for accessibility.

### 2. Impact & Backtest Metric Tags
- **Metric Wells:** High-contrast tags rendered inside `#F1F5F9` container blocks.
- **Predicted ROI Badges:** `#ECFDF5` fill, `#065F46` text, leading upward arrow vector (`+340% ROI`).
- **90-Day Data Replay Proof:** Outlined micro-badge with navy border and amber lightning glyph (`90D Replay Verified`).
- **Golden-Set Benchmark Score:** Navy container (`#0B192C`) with stark white tabular rating (`99.2/100 Accuracy`).

### 3. Active Roster Monitoring Cards
- **Header:** Worker designation, active integration icon (Square, Clover, Toast), and live heartbeat indicator (pulsing `#10B981` dot).
- **Calibration Confidence Dial:** Semi-circular mini SVG gauge mapping calibration from 0 to 100%. Color transitions from Amber (<80%) to Mint (>90%).
- **Fail-Safe Mechanism:** Distinct, outlined "One-Click Fire" button with `#EF4444` hover tint, requiring a 1-second long-press confirmation to prevent accidental disconnection.

### 4. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Hire / Confirm):** High-energy amber background (`#FF8A00`), dark navy text (`#0B192C`), `Space Grotesk` semibold, slight tactile inset active state (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (Shadow Shift / Details):** Navy background (`#0B192C`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Slate ghost button with border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, focus state shifts border to `#FF8A00` with an amber focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.2)`). Tabular placeholder text for numerical fields.

### 6. Mobile Bottom Dock
- Fixed slate/navy floating container (`#0B192C`) with frosted glass blur. Houses icons for `Decision Deck`, `Active Roster`, `Impact Analytics`, and `Settings`, with orange vector indicators under the active screen.
