# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic styled with a modern **fidelity** variant, utilizing the **Inter** font family across headlines, body copy, and labels. The overall tone is professional, dependable, and highly structured.

## Colors
- **Primary:** `#1234e3`
- **Secondary:** `#6671be`
- **Tertiary:** `#9e1c00`
- **Neutral:** `#767682`
- **Mode:** Light

## Typography
All text elements utilize **Inter**, offering exceptional legibility across all form factors.

## Layout & Spacing
Built on a proportional spacing scale (level 2) with standard `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth
Relies on clean tonal separation and low-opacity ambient shadows.

## Shapes
Rounded elements (`roundedness: 2`) utilizing a 0.5rem base radius for standard components.

## Components
Consistent styling applied to buttons, cards, inputs, and navigation elements.
