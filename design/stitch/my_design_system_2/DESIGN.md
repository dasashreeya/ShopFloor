# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system follows a modern, reliable, and professional Corporate / Modern aesthetic. It leverages a clean, structured layout with a focus on usability, clarity, and precision.

## Colors
- **Primary Color:** `#1234e3`
- **Secondary Color:** `#99a9c6`
- **Color Mode:** Light

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter (headline, body, and label)

## Layout & Spacing
- **Spacing Scale:** Standard 8px/16px rhythm with consistent gutters and margins.

## Elevation & Depth
- Uses subtle tonal layering and soft shadows for clear depth separation.

## Shapes
- **Roundedness:** Level 2 (Rounded, 0.5rem base radius).

## Components
- Standardized buttons, inputs, cards, and chips built to match the Inter typography and rounded shape language.
